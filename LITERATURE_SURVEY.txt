## Gap Analysis

Existing OCR, few-shot vision, multilingual NLP, and forecasting papers solve important subproblems, but they are usually evaluated separately. A small shop needs a fused system: handwriting, product images, and informal chats must become one consistent business database. Synapse ERP contributes this integration layer while keeping the model claims honest: the OCR, NER/product, and forecasting modules are the trained DL components, while ERP generation is treated as software orchestration.

## Justification for Chosen Approach

CRNN + CTC is selected as the OCR baseline because it can learn from line-level labels without character box annotations. TrOCR is the planned upgrade because transformer pretraining helps when handwriting data is limited. MuRIL/IndicBERT are selected because Indian merchants often use code-mixed and transliterated language. Prototypical metric learning is selected because shopkeepers may provide only one or two product reference photos. LSTM forecasting is selected as the first trainable intelligence model because it directly maps digitized transaction history to inventory decisions and satisfies the course RNN/LSTM requirement.
