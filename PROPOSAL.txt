## Objective

The objective is to build a deep-learning system that converts the informal business records of small Indian shops into structured digital records. The expected outcome is a working pipeline that ingests handwritten ledger lines, product references, and WhatsApp-style orders, then generates inventory, customer CRM, invoices, and demand analytics.

## Problem

Many MSMEs run on paper khata ledgers, shelf photos, and chat orders. The data exists, but it is unstructured and difficult for normal ERP software to process. Manual typing is the key friction point, so the project removes typing and focuses on perception models that read the existing records.

## Dataset Sources

| Data type | Source plan | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Handwritten ledger text | IIIT-HW, synthetic ledger-line images, and consented local samples | OCR training and character-level evaluation |
| Code-mixed chat orders | Synthetic Hinglish/Tanglish order corpus, later consented merchant chats | NER token classification |
| Product photos | Few-shot reference photos collected per SKU | Metric-learning prototypes |
| Transaction time series | Synthetic transactions, later digitized ledger outputs | Demand forecasting |

The repository starts with synthetic generators so the demo is reproducible without private data. Final model training should use at least 1000 samples or document the exception clearly.

## Methodology

1. Preprocess incoming records into model-ready tensors and normalized text.
2. Train a CRNN + BiLSTM + CTC ledger OCR baseline for unsegmented handwritten lines.
3. Fine-tune or integrate TrOCR for stronger Indic handwriting recognition.
4. Train a metric-learning product encoder with triplet/prototypical loss.
5. Fine-tune MuRIL or IndicBERT for code-mixed order NER using BIO tags.
6. Merge extracted entities into a business knowledge graph.
7. Train an LSTM demand forecaster and compare it with a moving-average baseline.
8. Generate an ERP dashboard with inventory, CRM, invoice, and analytics views.

## Architecture Overview

Inputs flow through three perception models: ledger OCR, product recognition, and order NER. Their outputs are normalized into shared SKU, customer, invoice, and transaction schemas. A business knowledge graph links equivalent items and customers. A forecast model predicts short-term SKU demand, and the ERP renderer exposes the data through a simple dashboard.

## Expected Outcome

The final project should include modular code, an architecture diagram, EDA/evaluation notebooks, training curves, metrics, saved model weights, and a live demo. The proof of concept should show: paper khata in, structured ERP out.
